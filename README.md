# ICT Mahidol Recruitment Landing Page

This website was created as part of our Week 14 Product Communication Campaign for ITLG311 Business Communication at Mahidol University.

## About the Project

Our campaign focuses on promoting the Faculty of ICT at Mahidol University to Chinese high school students who are interested in studying technology abroad.

The landing page introduces the ICT international programme and highlights three main features:

- English-taught ICT programme
- Eight ICT specializations
- Project-based learning

It also provides information to help prospective students explore the programme and admission requirements.
